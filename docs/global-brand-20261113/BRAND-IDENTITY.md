# Wiele Investments — global property identity

13 November 2026. Supersedes Malta-led brand positioning and “From South African roots to Malta shores. Thinking globally.”

## Position
Global property sales. South African roots. Based in Malta. International buyers, sellers and property ambitions lead the story.

## Brand language
- Primary descriptor: **Global property sales.**
- Signature: **South African roots. Global perspective.**
- Homepage headline: **Exceptional property. Global perspective.**
- Story: Wiele’s vision connects international buyers with property across countries and continents. Malta is the business base. South Africa is the heritage. Neither limits the brand’s outlook.
- Property direction: luxury apartments and penthouses, architectural villas, modern and traditional residences, wine estates and investment property.
- Buyer action: Tell us where you want to buy.
- Seller action: Introduce your property.
- Voice: assured, personal, clear, internationally minded. Architectural specificity and useful detail establish quality.

## Visual identity
Retain the approved reflective gold Wiele Investments wordmark, charcoal, ivory and champagne palette, Manrope and Cormorant typography. Express the change through the story, architecture and imagery. Avoid using a national skyline, island coordinates, flags or a map as the primary brand identity.

Photorealistic architectural editorial images; credible geometry, natural materials, controlled warm light, crisp detail, restrained styling. Mix contemporary architecture with distinctive traditional homes. Creative references: Italy, Spain, Dubai, Cape Town and London. Geographic inspiration is labelled as inspiration and never represented as a real location photograph or available listing.

Keep source-resolution generated originals. Deliver optimised WebP images to the website. Preserve conceptual-image disclosures in captions, credits and structured image metadata. Actual listings must use verified property imagery and particulars.

## Operational boundaries
International enquiries are welcome. Country coverage, stock, marketing authority, engagement terms and responsible licensed practitioners are confirmed for each brief. Do not claim a global office network, existing inventory or country-specific licensing. Wiele-managed maintenance remains Malta-only. The existing Malta report and Malta legal preparation retain their country and evidence dates.

## Website application
Home, buyer, seller, collection, About, Contact, onboarding, shared navigation/footer, FAQs, page metadata, image metadata, social share image, organisation schema, factual crawler summary and sitemap. Retain working enquiries, consent controls and registered access to articles and report downloads. No social account changes or paid campaigns are part of this website update.
