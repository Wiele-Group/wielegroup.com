# Research controls

Cutoff: 3 November 2026, Europe/Malta. Public-facing brand requested: Wiele Group; project identity Wiele Properties retained in supporting context. No publication or live website change authorised or performed.

EU quantitative evidence is not all geographical Europe. Selected peers are Southern European tourism markets and Cyprus as an island comparator; no returns ranking is inferred. Guest nights count people and duration, not property occupancy. NSO visitor expenditure is not accommodation revenue.

Two independent property research organisations provide broader housing context only. No institutional living or hotel yield is transferred to individual apartments. No verified locality ADR, occupancy, prices or financing offers were available, so none is asserted. Outlook is editorial interpretation and conditional scenarios, not an attributed numerical 2027 forecast.

Eurostat annual article contains an ambiguous EU/EFTA sentence; the article avoids that aggregate and uses the unambiguous EU Q2 monthly release. Spain's original 2025 national-register guidance is superseded in relevant part by 2026 court annulments; current consolidated text and published ruling were checked. France source used only for statutory municipal powers, not a claim that every municipality adopted a cap.

EU legislative text was challenged by a verification page on opening; indexed official provisions and current Commission operational guidance were cross-checked. Malta primary PDF downloaded and relevant Category E provisions read. Spain tax measure is scheduled rather than in force at cutoff; parliamentary status needs a final refresh before public release. MTA portal migration notice means application channel/lead times also need checking for a live acquisition.

Sources are linked beside claims. Official data are attributed; third-party research is paraphrased briefly. No data purchased, accounts created, or access controls bypassed. AI hero is illustrative and depicts no actual Wiele listing.
