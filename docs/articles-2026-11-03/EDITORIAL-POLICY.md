## Quarterly publication format — confirmed 3 November 2026

- Publish four substantive quarterly editions per year, identified by quarter and year. Focus on Malta income-producing investment real estate, established going concerns and properties with credible operating potential.
- Each edition covers: market conditions; relevant industry/business news; regulatory and policy updates; statistical releases; and other material developments affecting income, operating costs, financing, acquisition, licensing or exit prospects.
- Give each development a practical investor implication. Cover short lets and other relevant income-property segments where there is evidence; the series is not restricted to holiday accommodation.
- Compare new observations with the previous comparable quarter and the same period a year earlier where appropriate. Show the observation period, publication date, research cutoff, source and any revisions. Do not label a partial quarter as a completed quarter or an older observation as current-quarter performance.
- Prioritise NSO Malta, Central Bank of Malta, Eurostat, ECB, current official legislation and competent authorities. Use independent property research for context, with scope differences disclosed.
- Separate enacted/commenced law, scheduled changes and proposals. Explain licence/transfer implications for going concerns without implying that business rights transfer automatically.
- Include charts or editorial imagery when they improve understanding. Keep measured outcomes, actual/contracted income, projections and illustrative scenarios distinct.
- End with a measured outlook for the next quarter and the following 12 months, identifying assumptions, risks and the evidence that would change the view.
- Suggested series title: Wiele Quarterly — Malta Investment Property Update | Q[1–4] [year].
- The November 2026 launch article remains dated 3 November; it is not retrospectively presented as a completed Q4 review. Future quarterly editions should use the latest available releases and disclose reporting lags.
- This records an editorial cadence. No automatic research, publishing job, reminder or email distribution has been scheduled by this update.
