# Wiele listing presentation research

Reviewed 15 November 2026. Objective: show prospective sellers how their own assets could be presented. The firms below are established category benchmarks selected for relevance and public listing detail, not an independently audited ranking by turnover, transaction value or customer outcomes. Automotive examples include dealers and auction/private-sale houses, whose models differ from a brokerage. No affiliation or comparable Wiele track record is implied.

| Category | Benchmark and source | Observed approach | Wiele adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|
| International property | [Knight Frank](https://www.knightfrank.com/property-for-sale) | International location and property-type discovery, visual collection and specialist consultation | Five distinct property examples, location/type early, clear buyer-facing facts and seller enquiry |
| International property | [Sotheby’s International Realty](https://www.sothebysrealty.com/eng/sales/int) | Search result shows location, asking currency, beds, baths, internal size and marketing office | Separate internal/outdoor area, room counts, currency and identity. Search result text observed; direct page fetch returned 405, so detailed interactive behaviour was not verified |
| Yachts | [Burgess](https://www.burgessyachts.com/en/buy-a-yacht/yachts-for-sale) | Vessel-led discovery with length, build information and accommodation | Vessel hero, accommodation/specification summary, technical records and independent survey route |
| Yachts | [Edmiston](https://www.edmiston.com/yachts-for-sale/search/) | Filters for type, length, price, year, builder and guests; cards distinguish VAT basis, cabins and build/refit dates | Keep tax status beside price, distinguish build/refit and expose records. No blanket 18% VAT applied |
| Aircraft | [Jetcraft](https://www.jetcraft.com/aircraft/) | Inventory search and technical configuration, dated utilisation and specification documents | Hours/cycles separate, records before lifestyle performance claims, category enquiry |
| Aircraft | [Boutsen Aviation](https://www.boutsen.com/evaluation.php) | Aircraft input fields distinguish manufacturer, serial, year, base, registration, airframe time, engine/APU programmes, inspections, tracking and damage history | Separate aircraft identity/utilisation, maintenance evidence and import status; do not publish fabricated registrations |
| Exotic / performance cars | [Tom Hartley](https://www.tomhartley.com/used/) | Make/model, year, mileage with units, transmission, specification, price and availability | Distance units visible, model/specification and history separated, no invented warranty |

These observations inform design decisions, not evidence of conversion uplift. Competitor branding, listings, prices, copy and photographs are not reproduced.

## Recommended structure

1. Discovery card: large 16:10 photograph, unmistakable example badge, type, location, title, asking price/currency, three useful category-specific facts and a detail link.
2. Detail: disclosure before the title, reference, large hero, visible AI caption and jump navigation.
3. Overview: short asset story and three specific highlights.
4. Specifications: readable two-column definition list; distinguish internal/plot/outdoor areas, hours/cycles, guest/cabin capacity or odometer/mileage basis.
5. Records and condition: ownership/authority, applicable plans or technical records, defects, evidence status and independent review.
6. Costs and terms: price basis, tax/customs status, included items, running costs and material limits. No automatic VAT assumptions across countries or categories.
7. Enquiry: desktop side panel, normal mobile flow; clear seller enquiry prefilled with the chosen example. No reservation, purchase or invented brochure controls.
8. Related presentations and links to the existing purchase guides.

## Design decision

Retain Wiele’s approved ivory/charcoal/gold identity, Cormorant/Manrope fonts, navigation and existing disclosed AI concept assets. Two-column discovery cards allow readable specs; a final full-width card balances the five-property set. Specialist pages show one larger image/card. Detail pages pair a broad content column with a compact seller panel; mobile presents one column. Named sections and native disclosure controls work without hover. No heavy new animation or dependency.

All nine examples are fictional. Their sample figures do not describe the AI images. Asking prices are format demonstrations, not valuations or researched comparable prices. No actual manufacturer/model, asset identification, owner, tenancy, income or certification is fabricated. Wine-estate records distinguish real estate and operating-business scope; no production, revenue or yield is asserted.

## Discovery and metadata

Category pages remain indexable with useful location/service copy, canonical/social metadata and unchanged organisation identity. Example details have unique titles/descriptions, self-canonical URLs, image descriptions, WebPage/Breadcrumb/ImageObject data and noindex/follow. They are intentionally omitted from the public sitemap, with no Product, Offer, RealEstateListing or vehicle sale schema. llms.txt explicitly explains their fictional nature. Noindex is a directive and does not guarantee every crawler will comply.

Before real listings are added, obtain marketing authority, verified asset particulars, rights-cleared actual photography, measured/technical documentation, current price and tax basis, condition disclosures and jurisdiction-specific publication requirements. Replace fictional examples with separate real listing records rather than silently turning sample values into inventory.

## Registration requirement added by the owner

Full listing presentations require saved name/email registration. Public discovery cards expose only image, title, category and illustrative location. Prices, specifications, full descriptions and records live in a server-only dataset; these are not imported into client components or anonymous HTML/RSC. The signed listing-access cookie uses a distinct scope from existing article/report access, lasts 30 days in the registering browser and contains no name/email. The registration form uses existing Turnstile, Cloudflare storage and optional inbox notification patterns; marketing consent is unticked and independent. The privacy notice explains this processing. Full listing responses bypass shared caches with private/no-store headers. No live test registration or notification is sent.
