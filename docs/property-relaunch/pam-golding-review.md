# Premium direction review — 24 October 2026

Reference inspected: https://www.pamgolding.co.za/ (homepage, desktop browser and current page content).

## Observed reference and Wiele response
| Area | Pam Golding observation | Wiele revision |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Strong crest/wordmark presence against a forest field; sparse navigation over immersive photography/video | Use the user's gold-ring W with gold WIELE wordmark in a consistent forest masthead; own composition and typography |
| First impression | Full-screen property atmosphere precedes discovery | Directional image shading keeps the residence visible; left-aligned editorial headline; real enquiry paths directly beneath |
| Discovery | Luxury Collection and Lifestyle Living link to actual inventory | Image-led lifestyle brief links, clearly labelled concepts/location photography; no invented search results or stock |
| Rhythm | Large photographic areas and distinct content sections | Varied image heights, two-column editorial introduction, full-width seller feature, quiet collection note and structured approach |
| Trust | Real inventory, property facts, established agency claims | Named direct contact, agreed service scope and explicit empty collection; no borrowed credentials or performance claims |

Design judgement: Wiele's earlier centred headings, uniformly darkened hero, repeated imagery and small standalone wordmark weakened its identity and made the page repetitive. The new direction uses contrast, clearer composition and stronger brand presence. These are design judgements, not measured conversion results.

## Copy
- New homepage proposition: “A place apart. A life of your own.”
- Seller and buyer entry points describe the immediate action.
- Shorter, concrete paragraphs explain presentation, scope and the direct relationship with Jonathan.
- No “Property marketing” descriptor in the masthead. Detailed service scope remains in body/footer.
- No false listings, testimonials, track record, partner names or licensing claims added.
