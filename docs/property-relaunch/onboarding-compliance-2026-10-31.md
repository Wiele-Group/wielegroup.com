# Investor and seller onboarding — Malta

Research and implementation date: 31 October 2026 (Europe/Malta).

## Delivered scope

Two initial-enquiry pages, `/investor-onboarding` and `/seller-onboarding`, use the existing protected `/api/enquiry` workflow. They gather a structured brief and record the visitor's understanding of the process. They do not complete statutory CDD, clear eligibility, accept an agency instruction, reserve property or take payment. No document uploads, ID numbers, dates of birth, bank details or tenant information are requested. No automatic eligibility decision is made.

Investor fields: contact, residence country, Malta/Gozo locality, structure, use, budget range, funding route, acquisition-review status, timing, message and process acknowledgement. Seller fields: contact, locality, relationship/authority, property type, tenure, occupancy, EPC and planning-document status, timing, message and process acknowledgement. Unknown/pending options are available; only self-reported status is recorded.

## Operating actions still required outside the public form

- Confirm Wiele's licensing/capacity and actual activity against Cap. 644; identify the responsible practitioner and authority before regulated intermediation.
- Determine each relevant person's AML role. Maintain actual risk assessment, CDD, beneficial-owner, PEP/sanctions, funding, escalation, monitoring and record-retention procedures. A notary's work does not automatically discharge another subject person's duties.
- Agree a secure document-collection channel with access controls, processors, retention and lawful disclosures before asking for sensitive documents. This change does not create a document vault or compliance case-management system.
- Approve property marketing only after authority, particulars, EPC readiness and material limitations have been reviewed. Agree service terms, fees/VAT, duration and exclusivity separately.
- Obtain property-specific notarial, technical, tax and use/licensing advice. No legal opinion, practitioner registration verification or title certification was performed in this task.

## Data and technical behaviour

Existing Turnstile verification, origin restrictions, payload limits, honeypot, durable storage and inbox routing are retained. Onboarding data is validated against a strict investor/seller schema and must match the enquiry route. The website store retains enquiries for 90 days. Email correspondence can have a separate retention context disclosed in the notice. Formal AML files need their own legally assessed retention schedule; the website TTL must not be reused as that schedule. No real lead, marketing email, regulatory report or client message is sent during testing.

Success is returned only after durable storage. Notification failure retains the enquiry but does not establish email receipt. Tests use synthetic data with mocked storage, verification and notification providers. Live email receipt remains untested. The pre-existing Analytics receipt issue remains unresolved.
