# Premium visual direction — 24 October 2026

The user requested a Pam Golding inspired layout, stylish decorative premium property photography, a premium colour system informed by cognitive design and a high-end Wiele logo.

The final direction combines a full-width residential hero, restrained navigation, spacious introduction, collection section, three editorial lifestyle images and a seller feature. Wiele retains its own copy, composition and identity; no Pam Golding logos, listing photography, awards or copy are reused. Reference: https://www.pamgolding.co.za/.

## Design hierarchy
One primary action appears in the opening image. Related options are grouped below the introduction. Forest identifies primary actions on light fields; champagne provides contrast over the dark hero. Consistent spacing, legible labels and a short form reduce unnecessary decisions. These are design choices, not measured promises of subconscious influence, luxury perception or conversion uplift.

Static sRGB contrast checks: charcoal / ivory 13.31:1; muted text #606B63 / ivory 5.10:1; ivory / forest primary button 12.46:1; hero button text #1C2D26 / champagne 6.69:1; small accent text #766449 / ivory 5.23:1. Contrast over photographs depends on the crop and overlay and is reviewed visually. Champagne is not used for small text on ivory. This is not a full accessibility conformance audit.

## Decorative imagery
Four original OpenAI-generated photographs, with source masters and complete prompts in assets-source/property-photography. The fictional scenes serve brand atmosphere; none is an actual property offered for sale. Explicit disclosure appears on the homepage, footer and credits page. Future property listings must use actual authorised property photographs and verified details.

## Identity
Custom tapered W, spaced uppercase sans wordmark, deep forest / ivory / champagne. Outlined vector masters and transparent PNGs in public/property-identity/premium. Licence and usage notes in assets-source/property-identity. No trademark clearance is claimed.
