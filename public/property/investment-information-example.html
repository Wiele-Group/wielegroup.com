The asset.
The evidence behind it.

A structure for discussing a property and the information available. This is an unfilled example, not a property presentation or an investment recommendation.

Example only — no property offered.

No ownership, rental income, condition, value or availability is verified. Every property requires its own documents, sources, dates and material limitations. No return is promised.

02 / The income

Collected income

Rent actually received for a stated period, supported by records. Identify the period, source and any gaps.

Contracted rent

Rent in an agreement. Record relevant dates, lease terms, commitments and limitations. An agreement alone does not demonstrate receipt.

Projected income

An assumption with its basis and date. Keep it separate from collected or contracted rent. Do not present a projection as a guaranteed outcome.

Example status: no lease or income records supplied
