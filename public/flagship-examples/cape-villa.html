Architecture and title

The model assumes a single full-title residential erf in the City of Cape Town. The surface areas are stated separately so terraces and garages cannot inflate the internal price per square metre.

Tenure and proposed use
Full title. Owner occupation or one long residential lease. Confirm deed, servitudes, zoning and any consent with the conveyancer and municipality.
Areas
480 m² internal (5,167 sq ft), 84 m² terraces (904 sq ft), 54 m² garage (581 sq ft). Site 1,600 m² (17,222 sq ft). Garage and terraces excluded from 480 m².
Accommodation
Five bedrooms, four en suites, family bathroom, guest WC, separate study, utility, double garage and two off-street spaces.
Structure and services
Reinforced concrete frame, masonry infill, stone cladding, low-slope membrane roof, municipal utilities and battery-backed essential circuits. Step-free entrance on the arrival floor. Stair access between levels.
Orientation and exposures
Sea-facing living terrace with a wind-screened court behind. Marine corrosion, waterproofing, slope drainage and access after storms require inspection.
Scope of sale
Land, improvements and specified fixed fittings. Furniture, movable art and business goodwill excluded. Vacant possession assumed. No booking or letting history accompanies the example.

Title and drawings

Building fabric
Example visual finding: moisture staining at one terrace threshold. Trace the water path, open a test area with consent and obtain two scoped repair quotations.

Example visual finding: surface corrosion on exposed fixings. Obtain a façade specialist’s schedule. Request roof membrane, joinery and pool-service records.

Rental underwriting

A long-lease scenario uses monthly rent of R160,000 and 10% economic vacancy. There is no executed lease or collected income. Operating expenses exclude finance, income tax, capital expenditure and acquisition costs.

Potential gross rent
R160,000 × 12 = R1,920,000 / year. Gross scheduled yield 6.00% of the R32m price.
Vacancy and effective income
R192,000 vacancy allowance. Effective gross income R1,728,000.
Annual operating costs
Rates R204,000 + insurance R72,000 + repairs R156,000 + garden and pool R108,000 + management R96,000 = R636,000.
Net operating income
R1,728,000 − R636,000 = R1,092,000. Scenario cap rate 3.41% on price.
Vacancy sensitivity
At 5%, 10% and 20% vacancy, fixed-cost NOI equals R1,188,000, R1,092,000 and R900,000. Yield on price is 3.71%, 3.41% and 2.81%.
Price sensitivity
Capitalising R1,092,000 at assumed 3.0%, 3.5% and 4.0% implies R36.40m, R31.20m and R27.30m. These scenarios require local achieved sales and signed lease evidence before pricing a real instruction.

Buyer and seller routes

The property remains in South Africa. The purchaser’s residence, funding route and legal identity determine additional checks.

South African resident buyer
Confirm identity and source of funds through the conveyancer, inspect title and plans, agree finance and inspection conditions, obtain a duty assessment, sign transfer documents, then confirm Deeds Office registration.
UK or EU resident buyer
Arrange the inward transfer with a South African authorised dealer, retain inward-remittance evidence and obtain written borrowing capacity. Test the relevant exchange-control funding ratio against the buyer and purpose. Have the bank confirm timing and eventual remittance evidence.
South African resident seller
Provide title, bond cancellation details, approved plans, disclosure and certificates. Agree responsibility for rates, inspection exceptions and occupation. Reconcile tax, costs and net proceeds on the conveyancer’s completion statement.
Non-resident seller
Where section 35A applies above R2m, statutory withholding on the full price is 7.5% for an individual, 10% for a company and 15% for a trust, subject to a SARS directive. On R32m, the individual example is R2.4m advance withholding. The final tax follows assessment.
Handover
Release keys at the agreed contractual milestone. Record meter readings, fittings inventory and evidence of cleared settlement. Obtain local written advice before relying on the lease scenario.
