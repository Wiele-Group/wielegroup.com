# Wiele Property — approved Editorial identity

The user selected Option 2 (Editorial) on 24 October 2026. The approved identity is a high-contrast serif WIELE wordmark with a quiet, widely spaced sans-serif PROPERTY descriptor. The primary lockup contains no separate architectural icon.

## Palette and application
Forest #17332E, warm ivory #F8F5EF, champagne #C6AD86, charcoal #242B27. Use forest on light backgrounds and ivory over dark photography. Champagne is an optional dark-background collateral variant; do not use it for small text on ivory.

The wordmark is the primary brand identifier. The compact serif W is reserved for favicons and small avatars. Website width: 190 px desktop / 138 px phone; footer 205 px desktop / 180 px phone. Maintain clear space at least equal to the descriptor cap height. Do not stretch, simulate metallic effects, apply shadows or add a separate monogram to the primary wordmark.

The SVG letters are actual paths, with no external font dependencies or embedded bitmap. Optical reinforcement improves icon visibility but does not certify a universal minimum size. Print, foil, embroidery and signage need application-specific production proofs.

## Typography
Instrument Serif Regular (wordmark and W) and Geist Regular (descriptor), used under their SIL Open Font Licenses. Original licence texts are included as OFL.txt and Geist-OFL.txt. Font sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentserif and https://github.com/vercel/geist-font.
