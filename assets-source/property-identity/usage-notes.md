# Wiele Property — premium identity

Revised 24 October 2026 for the sales-only Malta property marketing platform.

A custom tapered W accompanies a light, widely spaced uppercase WIELE wordmark. The compact PROPERTY descriptor gives the name a clear category anchor. This replaces both the earlier archway identity and the heavier lowercase draft. The design direction is contemporary, restrained and premium.

## Current files
- Masters: public/property-identity/premium, actual outlined SVG paths.
- Primary and standalone marks: deep forest, warm ivory, charcoal and champagne.
- Signature: champagne monogram with ivory wordmark, for dark backgrounds.
- Transparent primary PNGs: 1760 × 600 pixels.
- Preview: identity-preview.svg and identity-preview.png.
- SVG favicon, square PNG website icons and ICO.
- Generator: scripts/build-property-identity.py. All letters are paths; no font installation is required to use the SVGs.
- Earlier identity files remain recoverable through Git; use the premium subfolder for current artwork.

## Palette and use
- Deep forest #17332E: brand fields, navigation and primary actions.
- Warm ivory #F8F5EF: reading fields and reversed artwork.
- Champagne #C6AD86: restrained accent and dark-background logo.
- Charcoal #242B27: body copy and monochrome mark.
- Stone #EAE3D8: quiet section separation.

Use the signature or ivory logo on dark photographs and forest backgrounds. Use forest on ivory. Champagne is unsuitable for small text on ivory. Keep clear space at least one PROPERTY descriptor height. Do not stretch, add gradients or shadows. Website primary width is 190 px desktop, 138 px phone. Small icon, print and embroidery minimums require application-specific inspection; no production certification is claimed.
