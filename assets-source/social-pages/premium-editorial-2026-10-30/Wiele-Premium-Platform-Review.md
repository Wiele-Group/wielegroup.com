# Wiele Properties — premium platform review and implementation
30 October 2026 · Europe/Malta

## Direction
A contemporary Malta property editorial: natural architectural light, warm ivory, charcoal, restrained champagne gold and a personal point of contact. Preserve the approved Option 1 identity and the original circle/W. Luxury is expressed through composition, legibility and a considered sequence rather than additional ornament. This is a design judgement, not audience research or a demonstrated conversion result.

## Findings and completed changes
| Finding | Applied refinement |
|---|---|
| Dark hero grading obscured the architectural image. | Split editorial opening: ivory copy panel and an unshaded, deliberately cropped architectural image. Original image pixels and AI classification retained. |
| Supporting logo lettering became too small in headers and wide covers. | Small-format digital adaptation uses the same original emblem and outlined Didot/Manrope lettering, with larger supporting type. Full centred Option 1 remains the master for report/footer contexts. |
| Report authority appeared late in the homepage sequence. | Report link in the opening view; publication feature moved ahead of the longer property and personal introduction sections. |
| Repeated dark boxes and competing interface elements added visual weight. | Quiet ivory journey links, consistent image heights, open text-led personal introduction, restrained borders and reduced hover zoom. Motion preference moved into the footer. |
| Report sources, table rows and form notes were small. | Larger web evidence labels, source notes, operating-yield table text and form instructions. Inputs remain 16px for comfortable mobile use. |
| Visitor preference controls lacked clarity. | More compact optional-consent panel with equal rejection/acceptance controls; readable analytics-setting colour; labelled motion control. |
| Social cover signatures were undersized. | Facebook and LinkedIn covers rebuilt around the readable wide signature and natural architectural crop, saved and inspected on the live profiles. |
| Social profile text was dense and the report action weak. | Facebook bio simplified; Instagram bio now explicitly points to report and enquiries. Existing contact and service scope retained. |
| Instagram has no published content. | Coordinated three-image editorial set and finished captions prepared for review. No standalone posts published. |

## Photography and image integrity
The existing exterior concept has consistent warm directional light, useful limestone/glass material contrast and a modern residential subject. Its previous dark wash reduced those strengths. The new composition removes that wash and gives architecture a dedicated image area; responsive crops preserve the principal façade and terrace relationships. This is presentation/crop work, not a claim of new photographic capture or retouching.

The medium-format aesthetic reference informed restraint, natural tonal relationships, believable perspective and material detail. No Hasselblad capture, RAW file, camera metadata or new original Malta photograph is claimed. Existing stock and AI classifications remain visible. No personal photograph is used. An original Malta architectural shoot remains the next imagery priority; the existing permission/shot brief remains applicable.

## Identity integrity
The original circle/W bitmap is embedded unchanged in the new hybrid SVG. Names, colours, wording and typography remain derived from approved Option 1. The digital composition is a responsive adaptation for legibility, not a replacement for the approved centred master. SVG supporting text is outlined; the emblem is raster. Font software is not redistributed.
