# Wiele digital photography and crop standards
30 October 2026

Use contemporary Malta residential architecture, pale natural limestone, believable window/interior balance and material detail. Keep verticals deliberate and avoid exaggerated wide-angle space. Preserve the source scene’s sun direction, contact shadows, building relationships and outlook. Prefer a strong exterior, a complementary interior and a material detail, rather than repeated crops of one hero.

Homepage desktop: image receives a dedicated 55% column; crop centres around the principal façade at approximately 63% horizontal alignment. Mobile: copy is presented first, followed by a 460px-tall image area (400px on the smallest breakpoint). The architectural photograph is not darkened to carry text. Captions sit on a separate readable surface.

Facebook/LinkedIn: retain an architectural scene on the left and brand field on charcoal to the right. Keep the main building and terraces intact within the crop. Use the small-format signature where the full centred signature becomes illegible. Keep generated-image disclosure in the image and the platform description.

For actual property instructions, use permission-cleared photographs of the actual asset and genuine outlook. Record capture date, locality, rights and any changes. Stock and AI concepts remain illustrative. No portrait is required. No camera provenance is inferred from style.
