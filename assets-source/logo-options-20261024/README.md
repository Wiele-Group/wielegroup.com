# Wiele Property: three premium logo directions

Concepts created 24 October 2026. These are original native vector constructions, not generated raster logos. No concept has been selected or applied to the live site.

1. SCULPT: a tapered ribbon W and spaced uppercase sans wordmark. Contemporary, restrained, strongest continuity with the current brand. The letter-based icon is useful for avatars and signage; its thin right terminal needs further optical adjustment if selected for very small reproduction.
2. EDITORIAL: a high-contrast serif wordmark with quiet sans descriptor. Most overtly fashion / boutique luxury. The full name carries the identity; a serif W compact mark is included for exploration but loses fine detail at 16 px and needs a dedicated small-size cut before production.
3. ATELIER: two offset geometric corners suggesting a private threshold / abstract plan, with a contemporary lowercase wordmark. More architectural and design-led; its abstract emblem needs consistent association with the Wiele name.

All three use the same deep forest #17332E, warm ivory #F8F5EF, champagne #C6AD86 and charcoal #242B27 so differences can be judged by form rather than colour alone. Champagne is shown as flat colour, not simulated metallic foil.

Files: three-directions.png/svg comparison; individual preview PNG/SVG; primary, horizontal and mark-only SVG variants in four colours; transparent primary forest PNGs at 1800 × 900. Lettering in SVG files is outlined, with no external font or raster dependency. Working source: build-options.py.

Typography: Geist (350 / 450) and Instrument Serif. SIL OFL texts are included. Source font files remain in the existing website project. SVG recipients do not need installed fonts.

Research checked on 24 October 2026: Pentagram's own current luxury real-estate case studies show restrained typography and neutral presentation at 520 Fifth Avenue, and serif wordmark / monogram with bolder character at One Wall Street. These informed the breadth of directions, not copied artwork or any claim that one trend guarantees effectiveness.
